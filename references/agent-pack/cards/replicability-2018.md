# Replicability across two studies

Bogomolov and Heller (2018), *Assessing replicability of findings across two
studies of multiple features*, Biometrika 105, 505–516.
DOI: 10.1093/biomet/asy029. Relevant PDF pages: 2, 4–5, and 10.

The paper develops procedures that first select promising features separately
in two studies and then test features selected in both. Its target is overlapping
signals, not merely an effect in at least one study. It provides procedures for
FWER or FDR on replicability claims, and discusses directional alternatives.

The guarantees depend on the particular procedure. They involve across-study
independence or stated weaker conditions; FDR results also involve conditions on
selection stability and within-study dependence. The paper includes modifications
for more general dependence. Do not infer a guarantee for any two screening rules
simply because the studies are separate.

A single agent with only source observations cannot calculate multi-study
replicability evidence from this paper. It can use the distinction between
selection and confirmation when reasoning about a frozen target analysis. Actual
replicability claims require the downstream information and procedure specified by
the study controller; they are not an interpretation of an exploratory source
p-value.
