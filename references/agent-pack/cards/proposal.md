# Research motivation: independent teams and heterogeneous subgroups

*NSF-BSF: Cross-Screening to Simultaneously Explore, Confirm, and Replicate
Findings*, Project Description. Relevant PDF pages: 6–9. This card is a sanitized
conceptual summary, not an excerpt from the proposal's experiments or results.

The proposal motivates exploration by several independent teams, each observing a
different subgroup, followed by computer-executed confirmation. It distinguishes
finding an effect somewhere from establishing effects in multiple subgroups. It
also considers objectives that reward correct lower bounds on the number of
subgroups with an effect.

Possible extensions include applying a design to data from several other groups,
or selecting/aggregating designs learned outside a held-out target group. Such
extensions require a specified aggregation procedure and its own error-control
argument. They are not part of the present session's interface or transfer cycle.

An important design challenge is heterogeneity: patterns observed in a source
subgroup may not identify which hypotheses are useful in another subgroup. A
design therefore trades focused testing against opportunities for target-specific
discoveries. The proposal motivates studying that tradeoff; it does not prescribe
a particular shortlist or weighting rule for your observations.

Only this conceptual overview is available to agents. The full proposal, its
preliminary simulations, numerical results, and figures are deliberately excluded.
